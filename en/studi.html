What it can do for you

Three examples of how an assistant can lighten the daily work of a practice.

Bookings

Someone writes asking for an appointment: the assistant collects the information needed and suggests the available times. You are the one who confirms.

Reminders

A message before the appointment helps people remember it and cuts down last-minute cancellations.

After-hours answers and follow-up

Opening hours, address, documents to bring: the assistant answers in the evening and at weekends, and leaves the requests that need a person to you.

These are examples, not a catalogue: every practice is different and we always start from a conversation.

Your data stays yours

Two free tools that run entirely in your browser: whatever you enter is never sent to any server.

Paste Sanitizer

Hides personal data and secret codes from a text before you paste it into an AI chat, a ticket or a forum.

Metadata Scrubber

Shows what your photos, PDFs and Office files give away (GPS position, author, company) and lets you download a clean copy.

The code is public on GitHub. The Metadata Scrubber opens on GitHub Pages.
